About

I’m a backend engineer in Islamabad. For about five years I’ve built the services behind real products: a multi-site sauna booking platform in London, a Qur’an reading app, the ERP and customer app for a Pakistani internet provider, real estate listings, and restaurant and WhatsApp commerce.

Most of my work is in NestJS and TypeScript, with PostgreSQL, Redis, RabbitMQ, and Docker. I like the parts of a system people rarely see: bookings that never double up, payments and refunds that reconcile, and queues that keep running when traffic spikes.

Right now I’m a Software Engineer at Transworld, working on the ERP and the backend behind the My Transworld app.
